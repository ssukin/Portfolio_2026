PLATFORM HOME — ASSETS, SOLUTIONS, PARTNERS, AND STORIES IN ONE PLACE
01  SITUATION

With no central database, teams were reinventing the wheel

We wanted to connect experts and assets — code repositories, whitepapers, demos — to the teams who needed them. Employees didn't know who to contact, and knowledge stayed siloed inside the accounts that produced it.

THE TASK

Develop a company-wide platform to leverage existing assets, accelerate delivery, and ensure clients receive the best solutions.

02  RESEARCH

Experts, and their time, are the scarcest resource

Interviews showed that people wanted whitepapers to learn on demand before booking time with a busy expert so the meeting was worth having, and identifying experts is not intuitive.

A comparative analysis identified standard features and materials for initial testing, as well as access methods on the market.

“I know who the expert is because I built my network over time, but I don't know what someone new would do.”
INTERVIEWEE

Old information is worse than no information

Interviews showed that people didn't use existing resource sites because content was viewed as historical rather than maintained. Maintenance was typically done by non-technical staff, so custom coding was a blocker.

03  DESIGN

Four object types, one config platform

I configured a Salesforce site that prioritized ease of maintenance for non-technical staff to ensure it stayed up-to-date.

The site serves as a single entry point for the top resources: ready-to-go solutions to share with clients, and associated assets such as whitepapers and code repositories, as well as articles from experts including contact information to learn more.

MY BILL — AMOUNT DUE, MONTH-OVER-MONTH COMPARISON, AND LINE DETAIL ON ONE SCREEN
01  SITUATION

Nobody understood the bill. They didn't want to keep paying blindly.

Terms and charges were hard to parse. Two questions came up in every conversation and neither had an answer on the page: why did this change since last month, and is what I'm paying reasonable compared to teams like mine?

THE TASK

Simplify bills for all points of contact, make charges clear, and align everything to TBM taxonomy.

02  RESEARCH

Utility bills already solved this

Competitive analysis of consumer billing, such as Pepco, pointed to three things this system had none of: bill-to-bill comparison, help text at the point of confusion, and interactive dashboards to break down the data. I turned competitor bills and TBM taxonomy into a requirements list, then ran card sorting to find groupings that matched how people actually think about their costs.

HOMEPAGE
01  SITUATION

A few minutes per candidate, and a site that made them hunt

My old portfolio was a Wix template that prioritized the back-end edits, not the front-end view.

THE TASK

Get a hiring manager from first click to a confident decision.

02  RESEARCH

Define the job before designing the page

I wrote a persona, positioning statement, jobs-to-be-done, major epics, and child stories. Then, I mapped user actions and behaviors to tangible metrics.

PERSONA

John, a busy senior manager. They want someone reliable, visually thoughtful, and good across functions. 

ACTIONS & METRICS
01
Arrive
A qualified reviewer reaches the site
02
Orient
80% of users can name my role and work within 60 seconds
03
Enter
80% of users open a relevant project within 60 seconds
04
Review
60% of users finish a project and can name my contribution and result
05
Act
60% of users take a second step: another project, the resume, or contact

Learn from comparables

I compared three families of sites: long-form case studies, like my old portfolio, up-front visuals, like Dribbble, Instagram, and Behance, and data-heavy job sites, like LinkedIn.

05  TESTING

Reviewers wanted everything, and fast

I ran moderated think-aloud sessions with people who review portfolios for a living. Five tasks, each scored true or false: find my role, pick a project, find the result, reach a second project, and connect my background to the work.
